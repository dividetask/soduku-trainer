# Glossary

Vocabulary only. Rules, fields and defaults live in the design docs.

- **Application (of a method)** — One use of a solving method on the board:
  one naked pair, one X-Wing, one single placed.
- **Box** — One of the nine 3×3 regions of the board.
- **Candidate** — A digit a cell can still hold.
- **Drill** — One Trainer board: a position where the chosen method is the
  easiest next step, played until the method finds nothing more.
- **Given** — A digit printed in the puzzle; it can't be changed.
- **Guess color** — Any color other than blue (Solve); entries in a guess
  color aren't checked and may be wrong.
- **Hint** — The Hint button's step-by-step help: technique name, then the
  pattern's cells, then the animation (or the result at once).
- **Lesson** — The main-menu button that opens the Trainer.
- **Line** — A row or a column.
- **Method / technique** — A named solving pattern (Hidden Single, X-Wing,
  …); the two words mean the same thing.
- **Mistake** — A wrong value, or a cell whose notes no longer include its
  answer.
- **Note** — A candidate the player has pencilled into a cell.
- **Single** — A placement found by Hidden Single or Naked Single.
- **Solve color** — Blue; entries in it count as the player's answer.
- **Sweep (Candidate Sweep)** — Filling in every cell's candidates and
  removing those ruled out by placed digits.
- **Trainer** — The part of the app that drills one method at a time.
- **Unit** — A row, column or box.
